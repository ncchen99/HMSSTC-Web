## FORMOSAT-7 (COSMIC-2)

FORMOSAT-7 (COSMIC-2) is a Taiwan-U.S. collaborative meteorological satellite constellation mission. The constellation consists of six microsatellites and provides high-quality atmospheric temperature and humidity observations for global weather forecasting.

### Mission Overview

- **Launch Date**: June 25, 2019
- **Launch Vehicle**: SpaceX Falcon Heavy
- **Number of Satellites**: 6
- **Orbit Altitude**: 550 km (low-inclination orbit)

### Scientific Outcomes

- Delivers more than 5,000 atmospheric profile measurements per day
- Significantly improves tropical weather forecast accuracy
- Provides near-real-time data to 100+ weather centers worldwide
- Supports applications including typhoon forecasting and climate research

### Center’s Role

The Han-Min Hsu Center for Space Science and Technology contributes to COSMIC-2 through scientific data analysis and application research, while also training talent in related fields.
