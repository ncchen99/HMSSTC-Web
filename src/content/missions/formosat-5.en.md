## FORMOSAT-5

FORMOSAT-5 is Taiwan’s first domestically developed optical remote sensing satellite. It carries the Advanced Ionospheric Probe (AIP) and was independently designed and manufactured by the Taiwan Space Agency.

### Mission Overview

- **Launch Date**: August 25, 2017
- **Launch Vehicle**: SpaceX Falcon 9
- **Orbit Altitude**: 720 km
- **Mission Type**: Optical remote sensing and scientific payload operations

### Scientific Payload

The Advanced Ionospheric Probe (AIP) onboard the satellite was developed with support from the Han-Min Hsu Center for Space Science and Technology. It measures ionospheric plasma characteristics in space and contributes significantly to space weather research.

### Major Achievements

- Taiwan’s first fully domestically developed remote sensing satellite
- Operated beyond its expected service lifetime
- Provides high-resolution Earth observation imagery
- AIP payload continuously produces high-quality scientific data
