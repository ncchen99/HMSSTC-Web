## FORMOSAT-8

FORMOSAT-8 is Taiwan’s next-generation high-resolution optical remote sensing satellite program, planned to carry more advanced remote sensing instruments and scientific payloads.

### Mission Overview

- **Planned Launch**: 2025
- **Orbit Type**: Sun-synchronous orbit
- **Mission Type**: High-resolution optical remote sensing

### Program Highlights

- Higher-resolution optical remote sensing capability
- Integration of next-generation scientific payloads
- Strengthens Taiwan’s indigenous space technology capabilities

### Center Participation

The Han-Min Hsu Center for Space Science and Technology participates in payload development and calibration, providing key technical support for this mission.
