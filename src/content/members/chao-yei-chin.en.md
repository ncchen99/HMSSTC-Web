Chair Professor Emeritus in the Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics at National Cheng Kung University, and former Department Chair and Director of the Aerospace Technology Research Center. A leading scholar in combustion and propulsion in Taiwan, his work spans combustion, jet propulsion, and engineering acoustics. He is an Associate Fellow of the AIAA.

### Education

- Ph.D., Aerospace Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology, USA
- M.S., Mechanical Engineering, National Cheng Kung University
- B.S., Mechanical Engineering, National Cheng Kung University

### Career

- **2004–** Director, Aerospace Technology Research Center, NCKU
- **2002–** Chair Professor, Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics, NCKU
- **2001–2004** Department Chair, Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics, NCKU
- **1998** Visiting Professor, Department of Mechanical Engineering, UC Berkeley, USA
- **2006–** Editor-in-Chief, Journal of Aeronautics, Astronautics and Aviation

### Research Expertise

- Engineering Acoustics
- Jet Propulsion
- Combustion Heat Engines
- Aeronautical Engineering

### Honors

- AIAA Associate Fellow (2003)
- AASRC Fellow (2005)
- NSC Outstanding Research Award (2003)
- President, Combustion Institute / Combustion Society of the Republic of China (2004–2006)
- Chair Professor, NCKU (multiple renewals)
