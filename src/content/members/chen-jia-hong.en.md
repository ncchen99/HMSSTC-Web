Associate Professor in the Department of Earth Sciences at National Cheng Kung University, where he leads the Space Weather Lab. Combining satellite and ground-based observations, his research addresses data assimilation, ionospheric physics, and space weather — including earthquake- and space-weather-related ionospheric disturbances, GNSS ionospheric monitoring, and the impact of magnetic storms on positioning accuracy.

### Education

- Ph.D., Earth and Planetary Sciences, Kyoto University, Japan (2012)

### Career

- **2017–Present** Assistant / Associate Professor, Department of Earth Sciences, NCKU
- **2014–2017** Research Assistant, Department of Earth Sciences, NCKU
- **2012–2013** Postdoctoral Researcher, Department of Earth Sciences, NCKU

### Research Expertise

- Data Assimilation
- Ionospheric Physics
- Space Weather

### Research Impact

- h-index: 37; ~3,694 total citations; 87 scholarly works (incl. 81 journal articles).
- The Space Weather Lab studies earthquake- and space-weather-driven ionospheric disturbances, GPS/GNSS ionospheric monitoring, tropospheric delay forecasting, magnetic-storm effects on positioning, space-weather data assimilation models, and artificial ionospheric disturbances from rocket launches.
