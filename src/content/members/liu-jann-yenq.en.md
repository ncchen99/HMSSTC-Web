Professor in the Department of Space Science and Engineering at National Central University, with joint appointments at the Center for Astronautical Physics and Engineering and the Center for Space and Remote Sensing Research. An internationally recognized ionospheric physicist, he is known for his work on earthquake-ionosphere precursors. His research spans ionospheric physics, space physics, GPS applications, and ionospheric radio-wave sounding.

### Education

- Ph.D. in Physics, Utah State University, USA

### Research Expertise

- Ionospheric Physics
- Space Physics
- GPS Applications
- Ionospheric Radio-Wave Sounding

### Research Impact

- h-index: 61; ~13,783 total citations; 378 scholarly works (incl. 347 journal articles).
- Notable work includes satellite observations of earthquake-ionosphere precursors, seismic-related Doppler frequency shifts, machine-learning detection of electron-density profiles, and the effects of ionospheric disturbances on GNSS positioning accuracy.
