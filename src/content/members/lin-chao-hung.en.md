Professor in the Department of Geomatics at National Cheng Kung University. His expertise lies in 3D point cloud modeling, computer graphics, and digital geometry processing, combined with satellite remote sensing for geospatial information visualization and analysis. His recent work applies optical satellite imagery and neural networks to inland water-quality monitoring.

### Education

- Ph.D., Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering, NCKU (2004)

### Career

- **2014–Present** Professor, Department of Geomatics, NCKU

### Research Expertise

- Computer Graphics
- Digital Geometry Processing
- Geospatial Information Visualization and Analysis
- Satellite Remote Sensing

### Research Impact
