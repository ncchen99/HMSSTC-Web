Associate Professor in the Department of Geomatics at National Cheng Kung University. He specializes in satellite orbital dynamics and precise orbit determination, with research spanning GPS/GNSS, COSMIC/FORMOSAT radio-occultation observations, and the ionosphere. Recent topics include satellite orbit assessment, GNSS time synchronization, and solar radiation pressure modeling.

### Education

- Ph.D., Department of Civil Engineering, National Chiao Tung University

### Research Expertise

- Spatial Information
- Satellite Orbital Dynamics
- Remote Sensing
- Parameter Optimization
- Satellite Data Processing
