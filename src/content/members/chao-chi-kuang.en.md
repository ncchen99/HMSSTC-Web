Professor in the Department of Space Science and Engineering at National Central University and Director of the Center for Astronautical Physics and Engineering. He specializes in space missions and science-payload development, alongside ionospheric physics research. He has contributed to science payloads for ROCSAT-1 and the FORMOSAT series, currently leads the FORMOSAT-9 science payload development, and is developing deep-space radiation instruments for lunar science.

### Education

- Ph.D., Graduate Institute of Space Science, National Central University

### Research Expertise

- Space Missions
- Science Payloads
- Environmental Testing
- Ionospheric Physics

### Research Impact

- h-index: 17; ~932 total citations; 62 scholarly works (incl. 56 journal articles).
- Representative work includes ion-temperature and topside-ionosphere measurements on ROCSAT-1/FORMOSAT, FORMOSAT-5 AIP mission operations, FORMOSAT-9 science-payload development, and a deep-space radiation probe for lunar science.
