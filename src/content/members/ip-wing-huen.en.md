Academician of Academia Sinica, Professor at the Institute of Astronomy, National Central University, and Chair of the Taiwan Space Union (TSU). An internationally renowned planetary scientist, his research covers cometary physics, the origin of the solar system and planetary formation, solar-system plasma physics, and planetary atmospheres and exospheres. He has contributed to major international space missions, was among the early proponents of the Cassini-Huygens mission to Saturn, and participated in the OSIRIS camera team of ESA's Rosetta comet mission.

### Education

- Ph.D. in Applied Physics, University of California, San Diego (1974)

### Research Expertise

- Cometary physics
- Origin of the solar system and planetary formation
- Solar-system plasma physics
- Planetary atmospheres and exospheric systems

### Key Contributions & Impact

- One of the early proponents of the Cassini-Huygens mission to Saturn and Titan.
- Member of the OSIRIS science-payload team for ESA's Rosetta mission to Comet 67P/Churyumov-Gerasimenko.
- h-index: 78; ~29,703 total citations; 619 scholarly works (incl. 21 books).
- Currently leads the Taiwan Space Union Office and the Taiwan Top Science Student Project, and participates in NASA SSERVI-related programs.
