Professor and Vice Chair of the Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics at National Cheng Kung University, and Associate Director of the Institute of Space Systems Engineering. He specializes in propulsion, spray and atomization, and combustion, with research spanning methane combustion, diffusion and premixed flames, opposed-jet flames, and particle image velocimetry (PIV), and long-standing work on clean-energy engine development.

### Education

- Ph.D., Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics, NCKU
- M.S., Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics, NCKU
- B.S., Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics, NCKU

### Career

- **2019–Present** Professor, Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics (Institute of Space Systems Engineering), NCKU
- **2017–2019** Department Chair, Mechanical & Automation Engineering, Kao Yuan University
- **2014–2019** Director, Advanced Clean Energy Engine R&D Center, Kao Yuan University
- **2014–2019** Associate Professor, Mechanical & Automation Engineering, Kao Yuan University
- **2009–2014** Assistant Professor, Mechanical & Automation Engineering, Kao Yuan University
- **2008–2009** Assistant Researcher, Advanced Clean Energy Engine R&D Center

### Research Expertise

- Propulsion
- Spray and Atomization
- Combustion

### Research Impact

- h-index: 21; ~1,382 total citations; 64 scholarly works (incl. 47 journal articles).
- Served three terms as a board member of the Combustion Society of the Republic of China.
