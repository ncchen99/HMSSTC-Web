Professor in the Department of Physics at National Cheng Kung University. His research centers on upper-atmospheric electrical discharges and transient luminous events (sprites, blue jets), atmospheric electricity, and lightning physics, together with the development of space and astronomical instrumentation. He is a key member of the science team for the FORMOSAT-2 ISUAL instrument that imaged upper-atmospheric lightning.

### Education

- Ph.D., Institute of Physics and Astronomy, National Central University (2001)

### Career

- **2020–Present** Professor, Department of Physics, NCKU
- **2016–2020** Director, Institute of Space and Plasma Sciences, NCKU
- **2014–2020** Associate Professor, Institute of Space and Plasma Sciences, NCKU
- **2008–2014** Assistant Professor, Institute of Space and Plasma Sciences, NCKU
- **2007–2008** Assistant Research Professor, Plasma and Space Science Center, NCKU
- **2001–2006** Postdoctoral Researcher, Department of Physics, NCKU

### Research Expertise

- Lightning physics and upper-atmospheric discharges
- Transient luminous events (sprites, blue jets, elves)
- Atmospheric electricity
- Astronomical observation
- Space and astronomical instrumentation

### Research Impact

- h-index: 23; ~2,124 total citations; 87 scholarly works (incl. 81 journal articles).
- Research uses satellite and ground-based observations of upper-atmospheric electromagnetic phenomena, including terrestrial gamma-ray flashes, the ionospheric effects of lightning, and severe-weather impacts on atmospheric coupling.
