Professor and Chair of the Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics at National Cheng Kung University, and Director of the Institute of Space Systems Engineering. He specializes in satellite navigation and control, with prior research at Stanford University's GPS Laboratory and the U.S. Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) satellite navigation teams. His work covers navigation augmentation systems, signal processing, and robust distributed network systems.

### Education

- Ph.D., Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics, Stanford University, USA (2003)

### Career

- **2018–Present** Professor, Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics, NCKU
- **2010–2018** Associate Professor, Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics, NCKU
- **2004–2010** Assistant Professor, Department of Aeronautics and Astronautics, NCKU
- **2003** Senior Systems Engineer, FAA GPS TAC Satellite Navigation Product Teams, USA
- **1998–2003** Research Assistant, Stanford University GPS Research Laboratory

### Research Expertise

- Guidance, Navigation and Control
- Satellite-Based / Ground-Based Augmentation Systems (SBAS / GBAS)
- Wireless Communications
- Signal Processing
- Robust Distributed Network Systems

### Honors

- NCKU Teaching Excellence Award (2009–2010)
- ION GPS 2002 Student Paper Competition Winner (2002)
- AIAA Senior Member; IEEE Member; ION Member
