The 7th International Conference on GPS Radio Occultation (ICGPSRO) was held from October 28 to 30 at the Wang-Hong Building, National Cheng Kung University (NCKU). Experts and scholars from top research institutions in the US, Europe, and Japan gathered to discuss the latest advances in Global Navigation Satellite System Radio Occultation and Reflectometry (GNSS-RO/R) technology for meteorology, climate, and space weather research. Through academic dialogue, this event aims to enhance the global research community's understanding of Taiwan's contributions to occultation observation and space weather research, expanding international scientific influence.

![Group Photo](../images/news/icgpsro-2025-group.jpg)
*Group Photo*

ICGPSRO is a major international conference in the field of GNSS occultation and remote sensing. In 2025, it was co-hosted by the Hsia Han-min Space Technology Center (HMSSTC) at NCKU and the Global Aerospace Observation and Data Processing Center (GAODARC) at National Central University (NCU), with planning support from NCKU, NCU, the Taiwan Space Agency (TASA), and the University Corporation for Atmospheric Research (UCAR) in the US. It is one of the few large-scale international academic gatherings in Taiwan focused on space science.

![TASA Director General Jong-Shinn Wu giving a speech](../images/news/icgpsro-2025-tasa.jpg)
*TASA Director General Jong-Shinn Wu giving a speech*

The conference themes covered critical issues such as ionospheric variations, numerical weather prediction data assimilation, GNSS-R sea surface wind field observation, and applications for next-generation satellite missions. Experts in the international space and atmospheric science fields shared the latest results in GNSS remote sensing technology innovation, space weather monitoring data application, and forward-looking climate observation.

![NCKU Dean of R&D Chuan-Pu Liu](../images/news/icgpsro-2025-liou.jpg)
*NCKU Dean of R&D Chuan-Pu Liu*

In addition to keynote speeches and technical forums, the conference featured poster displays and a young scholars forum, providing opportunities for early-career researchers to engage directly with international experts. This diverse interaction aims to cultivate a new generation of scientific talent with expertise in GNSS observation, data analysis, and model application, driving the continued development of space science in Taiwan.

![NCKU Associate Vice President for International Affairs Wei-Hsun Chang](../images/news/icgpsro-2025-chang.jpg)
*NCKU Associate Vice President for International Affairs Wei-Hsun Chang*

Since the FORMOSAT-3 and FORMOSAT-7 missions, Taiwan has long played a key role in the global occultation observation field, providing more observation data than any other country and making significant contributions to weather forecasting and space weather monitoring.

The NCKU team has played a core role in both missions—participating in ionospheric data analysis and space weather forecasting model development during the FORMOSAT-3 era, and leading the discovery of low-latitude ionospheric anomalies triggered by Antarctic stratospheric warming using occultation observations during the FORMOSAT-7 mission. These research results were published in the top international journal *Geophysical Research Letters*.

![Professor Tiger Liu from National Central University](../images/news/icgpsro-2025-liu.jpg)
*Professor Tiger Liu from National Central University*

In recent years, the Hsia Han-min Space Technology Center at NCKU has actively invested in satellite development and space observation research, promoting interdisciplinary integration and international cooperation. Through this ICGPSRO international symposium, NCKU continues to expand its international influence in space science, deepening collaborative links with global research institutions and driving the development of next-generation satellite observation and space remote sensing missions.
