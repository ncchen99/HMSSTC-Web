## Our Mission

The Hsia Han-Min Space Science & Technology Center (HMSSTC) was established at National Cheng Kung University (NCKU) with the mission of advancing Taiwan's space technology research and talent development. We strive to become a key hub for space science research in the Asia-Pacific region.

Guided by our core principles of "interdisciplinary integration, international cooperation, talent cultivation, and technological innovation," the center combines the research capabilities of various NCKU colleges and has established deep collaborative networks with domestic and international academic institutions and space research organizations.

## Vision

Our three core visions:

- **🛰️ Research Excellence** — Advancing frontier space science research, developing innovative payload technologies, and enhancing Taiwan's international visibility in space technology.
- **🌏 International Cooperation** — Building partnerships with global space research institutions, participating in international space alliance missions and talent exchange programs.
- **🎓 Talent Development** — Cultivating interdisciplinary space technology talents, fostering space education from the grassroots level, and building future workforce for Taiwan's space industry.

## History

The center is named after former NCKU President Hsia Han-Min, in honor of his outstanding contributions to Cheng Kung University and Taiwan's higher education. During his tenure, President Hsia actively promoted academic internationalization and technological research and development, laying an important foundation for the university's growth.

Since its establishment, the center has participated in multiple national-level space missions, including the FORMOSAT satellite series programs. With 9 space missions to date, the center has built comprehensive R&D capabilities spanning satellite scientific payload design, ground station operations, and data analysis.
