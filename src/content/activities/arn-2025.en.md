Co-hosted by the Han-Min Hsu Center for Space Science and Technology and the College of Planning and Design at NCKU, together with Kyoto University’s Research Institute for Sustainable Humanosphere (RISH), ARN 2025 was held successfully from September 16 to 18, 2025, at NCKU.

A total of 96 participants attended, including scholars from Kyoto University, the University of Tokyo, Nagoya University, Academia Sinica, and other leading institutions in Taiwan and Japan.

### Core Theme

**Sustainability for the Humanosphere**

### Eight Key Topics

- Satellite payloads and space engineering
- Ionospheric variability and remote sensing
- Urban ecology and pest management
- Wireless communications and semiconductor technologies
- Timber architecture and sustainable design
- Green energy and biotechnology
- Space weather
- Emerging low-carbon biomass materials

### Collaboration Outcome

A tripartite international MOU was signed during the symposium, marking a new milestone in Taiwan–Japan collaboration in space technology and sustainability research.
