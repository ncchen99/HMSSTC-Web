Hosted by the Han-Min Hsu Center for Space Science and Technology at National Cheng Kung University, the 7th ICGPSRO is an important international academic event in the field of GNSS radio occultation.

### Topics

The symposium focused on the latest advances in GNSS radio occultation, including:

- Occultation inversion methodologies
- Atmospheric and ionospheric sounding
- Data quality control
- Applications in meteorology and climate research
- New constellation-based occultation applications

### Participation

Researchers, engineers, and industry representatives from around the world were invited to share scientific findings and practical experiences.

### International Collaboration

The conference promoted technical exchange and international collaboration in satellite remote sensing, ocean, and atmospheric monitoring.

For more information, visit: [ICGPSRO](https://hmsstc.ncku.edu.tw/ICGPSRO/)
