## 2022 Taiwan Space Union Annual Meeting and the 4th Satellite Science Workshop

This annual meeting was organized by the Taiwan Space Union with support from the Han-Min Hsu Center for Space Science and Technology at National Cheng Kung University, bringing together experts and scholars in space science.

### Purpose

The event promoted collaboration among domestic space research institutions and provided a platform to share the latest scientific achievements.

### Workshop Highlights

The 4th Satellite Science Workshop covered key topics including satellite design, manufacturing, and testing, offering participants opportunities for practical learning and technical exchange.

### Participating Organizations

Major space research centers and universities across Taiwan joined to discuss future directions in space technology.

For more information, visit: [Taiwan Space Union Annual Meeting](https://hmsstc.ncku.edu.tw/TSU2022/)
