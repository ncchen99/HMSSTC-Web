Hosted by the Han-Min Hsu Center for Space Science and Technology at National Cheng Kung University, the second NCKU SPACE FORUM invited international space agencies, researchers, and industry representatives to discuss innovation and future trends in the space sector.

### Key Focus Areas

The symposium integrated academic research and industrial applications across themes such as satellite technology, space engineering, and international collaboration.

### International Participation

The event attracted participation from space research centers, satellite manufacturers, and emerging space companies across Asia-Pacific, Europe, and North America.

### Expected Outcomes

The forum aimed to strengthen global space collaboration and enhance Taiwan’s presence in the international space industry.

For more information, visit: [NCKU SPACE FORUM](https://hmsstc.ncku.edu.tw/space-forum/)
